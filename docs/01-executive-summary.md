## Executive Summary

Product Name: \[TBD - working name: ProfileLink / Biofolio]

A block-based professional profile builder that combines the simplicity of Linktree with the depth of a traditional resume. Users can mix and match modular content blocks, use intent-based presets, and apply visual templates to create shareable, mobile-first professional profiles optimized for career advancement or client acquisition.

Value Proposition:

> "The only platform that lets you build a resume-style profile with link-in-bio simplicity — optimized for getting hired or getting booked."

Target Users:

* Job seekers and career changers
* Freelancers, consultants, and coaches
* Creatives building portfolios
* Small business owners
* Students and recent graduates
* Corporate professionals building personal brands
